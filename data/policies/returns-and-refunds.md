# Returns and Refunds Policy

Owner: Store Operations · Applies to: all NovaMart stores and online orders returned in store.

## Return windows by category
The return window starts on the **delivery date** for online orders and the **purchase date** for in-store purchases.

| Category | Window | Condition required |
|---|---|---|
| General merchandise (home, kitchen, toys, sports) | 30 days | Unused or like new, all parts included |
| Small appliances (blenders, coffee makers, air fryers) | 30 days if unopened; **14 days if opened** | All parts included |
| Electronics (laptops, phones, tablets, headphones, TVs, cameras) | 15 days | Opened items carry a restocking fee (see below) |
| Apparel and footwear | 60 days | Unworn, original tags attached |

NovaRewards Gold members get **15 extra days** on every return window (see the NovaRewards policy).

## Restocking fee
Opened electronics returned for a non-defect reason (for example "changed my mind") carry a **15% restocking fee** on the item price. The fee is waived when the item is defective, damaged on arrival, or the wrong item was shipped.

## Items that cannot be returned
- Items marked **Final Sale** (clearance with a red "FS" tag).
- Gift cards and NovaMart store credit cards.
- Perishable food and opened personal care items.
- Items damaged by the customer (drops, liquid damage, missing parts).

Exceptions for wrong or damaged-on-arrival items are described in the Damaged, Defective and Wrong Items policy.

## Refund method
- Refunds go back to the **original payment method**.
- Cash purchases: cash refund up to $200 at the register; above $200 the customer receives store credit or a mailed check (customer's choice).
- Gift receipts: refund as NovaMart store credit only.
- Card refunds take 3–5 business days to appear on the customer's statement.

## Returns without a receipt
If there is no receipt and the order cannot be found by order number, card lookup or NovaRewards account, the store may issue **store credit** at the item's lowest selling price in the last 30 days, up to **$50**, with a valid photo ID. Above $50 requires a manager (see Manager Approvals and Escalation).

## Refund amounts that need approval
Any single refund above **$500** requires store manager approval before it is processed.
