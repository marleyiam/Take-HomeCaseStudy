# Online Orders, Shipping and Cancellations

Owner: E-commerce Operations

## Order numbers
Online order numbers use the format **NM-** followed by five digits (for example NM-10482).

## Shipping times
- Standard shipping: 3–7 business days. Free for orders over $49 and for NovaRewards Gold members.
- Express shipping: 1–2 business days, $14.99.
- Buy online, pick up in store: ready within 4 hours; held for 7 days.

## Cancelling an order
An order can be cancelled only while its status is **processing** (not yet shipped). Once an order is shipped it cannot be cancelled; the customer must return it after delivery.

## Delayed or lost orders
- If the carrier reports an exception (delay), wait until 3 business days past the estimated delivery date before taking action.
- After that, the order is considered lost: the employee can offer a replacement shipment or a full refund. No manager approval is needed for lost orders under $500.

## Delivered but not received
If tracking shows "delivered" but the customer did not receive the package, ask them to check with neighbors and wait 48 hours. After 48 hours, escalate to E-commerce Operations through the escalation process.
