# NovaRewards Loyalty Program

Owner: Marketing

## Tiers
- **Member** (free): 1 point per $1 spent.
- **Gold**: reached after spending $1,000 in a calendar year. 2 points per $1 spent, free standard shipping, and **15 extra days on all return windows**. The extension does not apply to Final Sale items.

## Points
- 100 points = $1 reward. Rewards are issued in $5 increments.
- Points expire 12 months after they are earned.
- Points are not earned on gift card purchases or NovaCare plans.
- When an item is returned, the points earned on it are removed from the account.

## Looking up a member
Members can be found by phone number or email at the POS. Employees must never read a member's full address or payment details aloud.

## Tier status
Gold status lasts for the rest of the calendar year in which it was reached and the whole following year.
