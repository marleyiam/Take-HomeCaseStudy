# Price Match Policy

Owner: Merchandising

## Who we match
NovaMart matches the current advertised price of an **identical item** (same brand, model number, size and color) sold by these major competitors: BigBox Depot, ShopRight, and the competitors' own websites. We also match **NovaMart.com** prices in store.

## What we don't match
- Marketplace or third-party sellers (for example sellers on Amazon Marketplace or eBay).
- Clearance, open-box, refurbished or liquidation prices.
- Bundle offers, "buy one get one" deals, rebates, or coupons.
- Prices from membership warehouse clubs.
- Black Friday and Cyber Monday doorbuster prices.

## How to verify
The customer shows the competitor's current price on their phone or in a printed ad. The employee confirms the item is in stock at the competitor and enters the match in the POS with reason code PM-01.

## After the purchase
If NovaMart lowers its own price within **14 days** of purchase, the customer can request a refund of the difference with the receipt. Competitor price drops after purchase are not matched.
