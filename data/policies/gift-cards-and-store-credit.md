# Gift Cards and Store Credit

Owner: Finance

## Gift cards
- Available from $10 to $500 per card.
- NovaMart gift cards **never expire** and have no fees.
- Gift cards cannot be returned, refunded, or exchanged for cash, except where state law requires cashing out balances under $5.
- Lost or stolen cards can be replaced only if the customer has the original purchase receipt showing the card number. The remaining balance is moved to a new card.

## Store credit
- Store credit is issued on a NovaMart store credit card and works like a gift card.
- Store credit never expires.
- Store credit cannot be converted to cash.

## Paying with gift cards
Gift cards can be combined with any other payment method. Gift cards cannot be used to buy other gift cards.
