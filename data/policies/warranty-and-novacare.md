# Warranty and NovaCare Protection Plans

Owner: Services

## Manufacturer warranty
Most electronics and appliances include a **1-year manufacturer warranty**. After the store return window has passed, the customer must contact the manufacturer directly. Employees can print the manufacturer's support contact from the product page in the POS.

## NovaCare protection plan
NovaCare is NovaMart's optional extended protection plan.
- Sold only at the time of purchase or within **30 days** of purchase.
- Price: 2-year plan is 12% of the item price; 3-year plan is 18%.
- Covers mechanical and electrical failures. The 3-year plan on electronics also covers one accidental damage claim (drops and spills).
- Claims are filed online at the NovaCare portal or by phone. **Stores do not process NovaCare repairs**, but employees can help the customer start a claim at the service desk.

## Cancelling NovaCare
A NovaCare plan can be cancelled for a full refund within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days the refund is pro-rated, minus any claims paid.

## Returning an item that has NovaCare
When an item with NovaCare is returned, the plan is cancelled and refunded automatically together with the item.
