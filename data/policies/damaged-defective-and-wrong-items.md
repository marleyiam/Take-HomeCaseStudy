# Damaged, Defective and Wrong Items Policy

Owner: Store Operations / Quality

## Damaged on arrival or wrong item shipped
If an online order arrives damaged, or the customer received the wrong item, the customer must report it within **14 days of delivery**. In that case:
- Full refund or replacement, customer's choice.
- No restocking fee, and this applies **even to Final Sale items**.
- Take a photo of the damage and attach it to the return in the POS.

After 14 days, treat the item as a normal return or as a defect.

## Defective items (stopped working)
- **Within the category return window**: full refund or exchange, no restocking fee.
- **After the return window**: the item cannot be returned to the store. Direct the customer to the manufacturer warranty or their NovaCare protection plan (see the Warranty and NovaCare policy).

## How to tell "defective" from "damaged by customer"
Defective means the item fails in normal use (does not power on, motor stops, screen flickers). Physical damage such as cracks, dents, liquid exposure or missing parts counts as customer damage and is not returnable. When unsure, ask the store manager to inspect the item.

## Safety recalls
Items under an official safety recall are always refunded in full, with or without a receipt, regardless of the return window. Check the recall list on the store intranet.
