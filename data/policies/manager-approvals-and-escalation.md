# Manager Approvals and Escalation

Owner: Store Operations

## Situations that need a store manager
- Any single refund above **$500**.
- A return up to **7 days past** the return window ("goodwill exception"). Only a manager can approve it; employees must not promise it to the customer.
- Returns without a receipt above $50.
- Any suspected fraud or a customer who has made more than 5 returns in 30 days.

## Situations that go to the operations team
- Policy questions that are not covered by the written policies.
- Delivered-but-not-received cases after 48 hours.
- System errors in the POS or order lookup.

## How to escalate
Open an escalation ticket with: the order number (if any), what the customer is asking for, the facts already checked (dates, condition, amounts), and what you recommend. Well-documented tickets are resolved faster — the operations team targets a response within 4 business hours.

## What to tell the customer
Explain that the request needs approval and give a realistic time frame. Never promise an outcome before it is approved.
